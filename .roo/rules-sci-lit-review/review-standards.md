# Literature Review Mode Rules

1. Search Strategy:

    - Document the search: which databases, which terms, which filters, which date
    - Use Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) appropriately
    - Search both keywords and controlled vocabulary (MeSH terms for PubMed)
    - Record the date the search was performed

2. PRISMA Compliance (for systematic reviews):

    - Record the number of results at each stage: identification → screening → eligibility → included
    - Document reasons for exclusion at the full-text review stage
    - Generate a PRISMA flow diagram

3. Quality Assessment:

    - For clinical studies: assess risk of bias (Cochrane RoB 2, ROBINS-I)
    - For observational studies: consider confounding, selection bias, measurement error
    - Note sample sizes and study designs — don't weigh all studies equally

4. Synthesis:

    - Organize findings by theme, not by paper
    - Distinguish between: consistent findings, contradictory findings, and understudied areas
    - When studies disagree, explore why (methods? population? power?)
    - Identify specific, actionable research gaps

5. Reference Management:
    - Every paper discussed must have a complete, accurate citation with DOI
    - Deduplicate across databases before reporting counts
    - Export results in the user's preferred format (BibTeX, RIS, CSV)
